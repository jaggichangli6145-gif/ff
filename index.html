📋 TOURNAMENT RULES

Read carefully before registering

👥
Squad Size
Each team must have exactly 4 players. 1 substitute is allowed per team and must be registered before Day 1.
📱
Device & Account
Players must use their own registered Free Fire account. Sharing accounts or using multiple IDs is strictly prohibited.
🚫
No Cheating
Use of hacks, mods, or any unauthorized third-party software results in immediate disqualification of the entire squad.
⏱️
Match Timing
Teams must be in the lobby 10 minutes before scheduled match time. No-shows after 5 minutes will forfeit the match.
🏅
Scoring System
Points = Kills × 2 + Placement points. Booyah gives 12pts, 2nd-3rd 9pts, 4th-6th 6pts, 7th-10th 3pts.
🤝
Fair Play
Toxic behavior, teaming with other squads, or stream-sniping results in point deduction or disqualification at organizer's discretion.
