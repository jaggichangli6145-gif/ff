📋 TOURNAMENT RULES

Read carefully before registering

👥
Squad Size
Each team must have exactly 4 players. 1 substitute allowed per team, must register before Day 1.
📱
Device & Account
Use your own registered Free Fire account. Sharing accounts or using multiple IDs is strictly banned.
🚫
No Cheating
Hacks, mods, or unauthorized software = immediate disqualification of the entire squad. Zero tolerance.
⏱️
Match Timing
Be in lobby 10 mins before match time. No-shows after 5 minutes will forfeit automatically.
🏅
Scoring System
Points = Kills × 2 + Placement. Booyah=12pts, 2nd-3rd=9pts, 4th-6th=6pts, 7th-10th=3pts.
🤝
Fair Play
Toxic behavior, teaming, or stream-sniping results in point deduction or disqualification.
